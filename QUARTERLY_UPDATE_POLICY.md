# Quarterly Knowledge Update Policy / 季度知识更新政策

- **Knowledge cutoff shown on the site:** 2026-08-22
- **Review cadence:** Quarterly, on January 1, April 1, July 1, and October 1.
- **Source priority:** Official specifications, vendor documentation, official product/pricing pages, first-party engineering posts, then reputable educational material.
- **Deduplication:** Normalize canonical URLs and concept names; update an existing item instead of adding near-duplicates.
- **Change safety:** Preserve a backup, use a focused diff, validate Chinese and English editions, run browser interaction tests, scan for secrets, then publish.
- **Volatile facts:** Prices, quotas, model availability, product names, and platform permissions must carry a verification date and must not be inferred when an official page is unavailable.
- **Translations:** Chinese and English must express the same facts. Product names and protocol identifiers remain literal.

- **网站标注的知识核实日期：** 2026-08-22
- **复核频率：** 每季度一次，计划在 1月1日、4月1日、7月1日和10月1日执行。
- **来源优先级：** 官方规范、厂商文档、官方产品/价格页、一手工程文章，其次是信誉良好的教育资料。
- **去重原则：** 规范化官方 URL 和概念名称；优先更新既有条目，不创建近似重复项。
- **变更安全：** 先备份，使用小范围 diff，同时验证中英文版本，运行浏览器交互测试与敏感信息扫描，再发布。
- **时效事实：** 价格、额度、模型可用性、产品名称和权限规则必须标注核实日期；官方页面不可访问时不得猜测。
- **翻译一致性：** 中英文事实保持一致；产品名、协议名和标识符原样保留。
